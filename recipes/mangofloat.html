Mango Float Recipe

This is a simple and delicious recipe for mango float, a popular Filipino dessert made with layers of graham crackers, sweetened cream, and ripe mangoes.

Home

Description

Mango float is a no-bake dessert that is easy to prepare and perfect for any occasion. The combination of creamy layers and fresh mangoes creates a delightful treat that everyone will enjoy.

Ingredients

Base

  • 1 package graham crackers
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • Ripe mangoes, (you can use about 2-3 ripe mangoes or more, diced)

Steps in Making Mango Float

  1. In a mixing bowl, whip the heavy cream until stiff peaks form.
  2. Gently fold in the sweetened condensed milk into the whipped cream until well combined.
  3. In a rectangular dish, layer graham crackers at the bottom.
  4. Spread a layer of the cream mixture over the graham crackers.
  5. Add a layer of diced mangoes on top of the cream.
  6. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of cream on top.
  7. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight to allow the flavors to meld and the dessert to set.
  8. Before serving, garnish with additional mango slices or whipped cream if desired.
